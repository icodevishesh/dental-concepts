        Talk with the lab
        Which scan files can I upload?

        STL, PLY, OBJ, DCM or 3OXZ exports from iTero, Trios and similar scanners. You can also zip the whole export and upload the ZIP. Include the opposing arch and the bite.

        Do I still need the RX form?

        Yes. Upload a completed RX form with every case. You can download a blank RX form, fill it in, then scan or photograph it.

        What about digital implant cases?

        Write the implant system and size on the RX form, and upload a full arch gingival scan taken without the healing abutment.

        I’m sending a physical impression. What do I do?

        Request a shipping label, and we’ll reply with the label and carrier details.

        Is the turnaround a guarantee?

        No. The summary shows the in-lab working days printed on our RX form. Weekends, holidays and shipping aren’t counted, so confirm timing with the lab before you book the patient.

        How should I name the patient?

        Use a patient ID or initials rather than the full name. The lab will match the case to your RX form.

        Prefer to talk it through?

        Call 1-800-700-LABS before you upload, and a technician will help you plan the case.
