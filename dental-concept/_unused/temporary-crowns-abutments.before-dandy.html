Overview

About temporary crowns & abutments

What it is

A temporary, or provisional, restoration is worn while the final one is planned and made. On implants, a temporary abutment and crown can also help shape the soft tissue before the final restoration.

Where it is commonly used

Dentists use provisionals to protect a prepared tooth, to trial shape and bite, and to bridge healing time in implant cases. Tell us what you want the temporary to achieve so it can be designed for that.

What to send

A completed RX with “Provisional” ticked, the shade, and your impression or scan. For implant temporaries, include the implant system and size.

Our RX form lists provisionals with restorations at 10 to 15 days. Timing for implant temporaries depends on the case, so confirm it with the lab.

Can I send a digital scan?

Yes. Tick “Digital imp scan” on the RX form. We do not have a public upload portal, so contact the lab to confirm how to send the files.

Can the lab supply the implant parts?

Yes. Tick “Lab to provide parts” on the RX form and write in the implant system and size.

Can the temporary follow a digital wax up?

Yes, that is a common reason to order one. See digital wax up and mention the plan on the RX.
