About custom shades

What it is

A custom shade appointment brings your patient to the lab so the person building the restoration sees the tooth in person, instead of working from a tab number alone.

When it helps

It is most useful for anterior teeth and single restorations that have to match their neighbours.

How to arrange it

Call (415) 558-9717 to book, and tick “Custom shade” on the RX form. Visits take approximately 15 to 20 minutes at 244 9th Street, San Francisco, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Turnaround times and case availability are not published for this service. Contact the lab to confirm current details before planning a case.
