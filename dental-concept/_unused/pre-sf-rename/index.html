Talk with the lab
What does Dental Concepts SF make?

Dental Concepts SF is a dental lab in San Francisco that makes full contour zirconia crowns, layered porcelain crowns, lithium disilicate (E.max) veneers, inlays, onlays and crowns, and gold crowns. We also make full arch and All-on-X prostheses, implant crowns, custom abutments, temporaries, digital wax-ups, printed models and custom shades. Browse lab services.

Where is your dental laboratory?

Our dental laboratory is at 244 9th Street, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA. We’re open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. Call 1-800-700-LABS (5227) to reach the team.

How long does a case take?

Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 days and implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks. Full contour zirconia is quicker, at 7 in-lab days for one to three units. Working days don’t include weekends, holidays or shipping, so confirm timing with the lab before you schedule the patient.

Do you offer rush service?

Yes, on most products, for up to 4 units. Pre-schedule it with the Lab Manager or Implant Coordinator at (415) 558-9716. Cases needed within 7 business days carry a $100 per unit surcharge, with an additional $100 per unit within 5 days. Anything under 3 days needs management approval and a quote.

Can I send a digital scan?

Yes. Tick “Digital imp scan” on the RX form. We work with scans from iTero, Trios and similar systems. Digital implant cases need a full arch gingival scan without the healing abutment. Contact the lab to confirm how to send your files.

How do I book a custom shade appointment?

Call (415) 558-9717. Shade visits take about 15 to 20 minutes at our lab on 9th Street. When your patient can’t come in, email shade photos to photos@dentalconceptssf.com instead.

What warranty do your restorations carry?

We repair or replace a failed final fixed restoration at no charge for materials and workmanship for up to one year (restrictions apply). Provisionals and auxiliary services are covered for six months, and implant abutments follow the implant company’s guidelines. The restoration needs to be returned to the lab.

How can a new doctor open an account?

Email accounts@dentalconceptssf.com or use our contact form. Then download the RX form, and you’re ready to send your first case.
