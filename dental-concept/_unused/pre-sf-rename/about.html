Our story

A dental laboratory history that starts with zirconia

Every crown we make carries a quiet goal: to give someone back the confidence to smile without thinking twice.

Dental Concepts began with zirconia. In 2006, partners Barry Johal and Silke Finis founded Prestige Milling Inc., one of the first Lava Milling Centers on the West Coast, making zirconia crowns for other dental labs.

As digital technology moved fast and dentists asked for more esthetic restorations, that milling work grew into Dental Concepts, a full restorative dental laboratory in San Francisco.

Today, German-certified dental technician Silke Finis leads our technical work, and entrepreneur Barry Johal leads the business. Together they’ve built a place where technicians enjoy the work, and you can see it in what leaves the bench.

The tools keep getting better, and we keep up with them. The goal has stayed the same since day one: give every patient the best restoration we can make.

Talk with the lab
When was Dental Concepts founded?

Our story began in 2006, when Barry Johal and Silke Finis founded Prestige Milling Inc., one of the first Lava Milling Centers on the West Coast. As demand for esthetic restorations grew, that milling work became Dental Concepts.

Who runs Dental Concepts?

German-certified dental technician Silke Finis leads the lab’s technical work, and entrepreneur Barry Johal leads the business. A team of experienced, certified dental technicians works alongside them.

Are your restorations made in the USA?

Yes. We design, make and finish restorations at our dental laboratory on 9th Street in San Francisco. Cases aren’t sent overseas.

What technology does the lab use?

We design on 3Shape, mill on Roland equipment and accept digital impressions from iTero, Trios and similar scanners. A technician finishes every case by hand.

Where is the lab, and when is it open?

244 9th Street, San Francisco, CA 94103. We’re open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM.

Can I schedule a custom shade visit?

Yes. Call (415) 558-9717 to book. Visits take about 15 to 20 minutes.
