About Dental Concepts

Dental Concepts is a dental laboratory at 244 9th Street, San Francisco. Founded in 2006 by technician Silke Finis and entrepreneur Barry Johal, the lab grew out of early zirconia milling work at Prestige Milling Inc. and today makes crowns and veneers, implant restorations, full arch prostheses, digital wax ups, printed models, and custom shade work for dental practices.
