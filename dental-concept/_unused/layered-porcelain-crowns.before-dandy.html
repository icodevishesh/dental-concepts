Overview

About layered porcelain crowns

What it is

A layered crown has ceramic built up by hand over a core. Working in layers lets the ceramist vary translucency, colour, and surface character across the tooth instead of relying on a single material.

Where it is commonly used

Dentists often prescribe it where appearance matters most, such as anterior teeth. Whether it suits a particular patient is a clinical decision, and we are glad to talk a case through before you prescribe.

What to send

A completed RX with “Zirconia (Layered)” ticked, the shade and stump shade, and your impression or scan with the opposing arch and bite. Photographs and a custom shade appointment make the biggest difference on these cases.

Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 days. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before scheduling the patient.

Talk with the lab
Can I coordinate shade preferences?

Contact the lab about shade preferences. Custom shade appointments can be arranged by phone.

Where do I find the laboratory RX?

The current prescription form is available on the RX Form page.

How long does it take?

Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 days. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before scheduling the patient.

Is anything extra needed for anterior cases?

Yes. Our RX form asks for a pre-op or study model with all anterior cases.

How do I tell the lab the shade?

Write the shade, and the stump shade where relevant, on the RX form. For shade-critical cases you can also send photographs to photos@dentalconceptssf.com or book a custom shade appointment.

Can I send a digital scan?

Yes. Tick “Digital imp scan” on the RX form. We do not have a public upload portal, so contact the lab to confirm how to send the files.

Can my patient visit the lab for shade matching?

Yes. Call (415) 558-9717 to arrange a custom shade appointment. Visits take approximately 15 to 20 minutes.

Case-specific questions?

We do not have a public case portal. Confirm the current submission method with the lab before sending patient records.
