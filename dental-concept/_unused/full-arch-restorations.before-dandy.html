Overview

About full arch restorations

What it is

A full arch restoration replaces all the teeth in one arch with a single prosthesis fixed to several implants. “All-on-X” refers to the number of implants supporting it.

How a case usually runs

These are multi-stage cases planned closely with the clinician, usually with a provisional before the final prosthesis. Talk to the lab early so records, try-ins, and timing can be agreed before the first appointment.

What to send

A completed RX with the implant system and size, and your records. For digital cases, our RX form asks for a full arch gingival scan without the healing abutment. Photographs help with tooth shape and shade.

Our RX form lists implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before scheduling the patient.

Talk with the lab
What should I confirm before sending a full arch case?

Contact the lab to discuss the case plan, required records, and current submission method.

Is there a public case portal?

No. Ask the lab for its current accepted route before sending patient records.

How long does it take?

Our RX form lists implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before scheduling the patient.

What do digital implant cases need?

A full arch gingival scan without the healing abutment, as noted on our RX form, plus the implant system and size.

Can the lab supply the implant parts?

Yes. Tick “Lab to provide parts” on the RX form and write in the implant system and size.

Do you make the temporary as well?

We make temporary crowns and abutments. Discuss the provisional stage with the lab when planning the case.

Who should I talk to before starting a full arch case?

Call the lab at 1-800-700-LABS (5227). It is worth agreeing the records and stages before the first appointment.

Case-specific questions?

We do not have a public case portal. Confirm the current submission method with the lab before sending patient records.
