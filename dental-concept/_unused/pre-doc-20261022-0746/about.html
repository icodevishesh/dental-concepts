Our story

About us

We not only change the color and shape of teeth, we change the smiles and confidence of those who once thought they could never smile again without hesitation.

Founded in 2006, partners Barry Johal and Silke Finis first made their name as Prestige Milling Inc., one of the first Lava Milling Centers on the West Coast, producing zirconia crowns for dental labs. As dental technology advanced and demand for aesthetic restorations grew, that work became Dental Concepts.

The demand for quality products and services is ever growing in the dental industry. Our team, led by German-certified technician Silke Finis and entrepreneur Barry Johal, focuses on producing the best quality restorations for our clients and their patients.

Using the latest technology available, we pride ourselves on delivering exactly what patients deserve: the best.

With our dedicated staff, we have created an excellent atmosphere for our team members to be productive and happy, and to make sure the final product delivered is the best it can be.

Our history

From early zirconia milling to a restorative lab

The work has changed with the technology. The focus on people and restorations has stayed close.

  1. Before 2006

    Among the West Coast’s first Lava Milling Centers

    Prestige Milling Inc. produced zirconia crowns for dental laboratories as digital milling began changing restorative dentistry.

  2. 2006

    A lab begins

    Partners Barry Johal and Silke Finis founded the laboratory, building on their earlier work at Prestige Milling.

  3. Today

    Dental Concepts

    The lab focuses on aesthetic and functional restorations, implant prostheses, digital wax ups, model printing, and custom shade appointments.
