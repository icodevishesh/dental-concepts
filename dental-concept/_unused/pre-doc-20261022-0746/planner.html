Is the turnaround a guarantee?

No. The planner shows the turnaround printed on our RX form. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before scheduling the patient.

Does the planner show prices?

No. Fees depend on the case. Ask the lab for current fees when you open an account or discuss a case.

Does the planner send anything to the lab?

No. Nothing you choose here is sent or stored. Use the RX form and contact the lab to start the case.
