How a case moves

From RX to delivery in four stages

Stage 1 of 4

Plan the case and complete the RX

Choose the restoration with your team, then fill in the two-page RX form: doctor details, restoration and implant choices, shade, and what you are sending with the case.

Download the RX form
Stage 2 of 4

Confirm how to send it

Call the lab to confirm accepted shipping or secure digital submission before you send any records. Request a shipping label and the lab replies with the label and carrier details.

Stage 3 of 4

Designed digitally, finished by hand

Cases are scanned and designed on 3Shape, milled on Roland equipment, and finished by a technician, with ceramic layered by hand where the restoration calls for it.

Lab services

Six ways we work with your practice

/01

Full contour zirconia crowns

Milled from a single piece of zirconia with no layered ceramic. Shape, contacts, and occlusion are designed digitally, then the crown is milled, sintered, and finished by a technician.

Get started
/02

Layered porcelain crowns

Ceramic built up by hand over a core, which gives the ceramist control over translucency for anterior and shade-critical work.

Get started
/03

Lithium disilicate (E.max)

Glass ceramic for veneers, inlays, onlays, and crowns, when you want strength and a natural look in one material.

Get started
/04

Implants & All-on-X

Full arch prostheses, implant crowns, custom abutments, and temporaries. Our RX form lists implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks.

Get started
/05

Digital wax up & model printing

Preview the planned result with a digital diagnostic wax up, and get printed models for the case.

Get started
/06

Custom shades

Bring your patient to 244 9th Street for a 15 to 20 minute shade visit, or email shade photographs to the lab.

Talk with the lab
What restoration services does Dental Concepts offer?

Full contour zirconia, layered porcelain, lithium disilicate, gold crowns, full arch restorations, implant crowns, custom abutments, temporary crowns and abutments, digital wax ups, model printing, and custom shades. Browse lab services.

How long does a case take?

Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 days and implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks. Try the case planner, and confirm timing with the lab before scheduling the patient.

Where can I download the lab’s RX form?

The current two-page prescription form is on the RX form page.

How do I submit a case or digital scan?

We do not have a public upload portal. Contact the lab to confirm the current accepted submission method before sending patient information.

Can I arrange a custom shade appointment?

Yes. Call (415) 558-9717 to arrange a custom shade visit. Visits take about 15 to 20 minutes.

Where should shade photographs go?

Email them to photos@dentalconceptssf.com.

How can a new doctor open an account?

Email accounts@dentalconceptssf.com or use the contact form.

When is the lab open?

Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, at 244 9th Street, San Francisco, CA 94103.
