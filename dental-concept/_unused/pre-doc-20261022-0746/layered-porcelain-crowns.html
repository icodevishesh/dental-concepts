How it works

From prescription to seat, with no guesswork.

Three steps, with a technician you can talk to at every one of them.

Prescribe

Tell us exactly what you need

Tick “Zirconia (Layered)” on the RX form, add the shade and send your impression or scan with the opposing arch and bite.

Design & make

Built up by hand, layer by layer

The core is milled, then a ceramist builds up the porcelain in layers to vary translucency and colour, and finishes the surface by hand.

Seat

Delivered ready for the chair

Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 days. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before you schedule the patient.

Crown options

A material for every case

Four crown options on one RX form. Material choice is a clinical decision, and we are glad to talk it through.

Discuss a case

Full contour zirconia crowns

Milled from a single piece of zirconia, with no layered ceramic.

  • Designed digitally, then milled, sintered and finished by a technician
  • Often prescribed for posterior teeth where strength is the priority
Learn more

Layered porcelain crowns

Ceramic built up by hand over a core for control over translucency.

  • Colour, translucency and surface character varied across the tooth
  • Often prescribed where appearance matters most, such as anterior teeth
You are here

Lithium disilicate (E.max)

A glass ceramic for veneers, inlays, onlays and crowns.

  • Natural, translucent appearance for single-tooth restorations
  • Include the stump shade: it matters with a translucent material
Learn more

Gold crowns

A cast gold crown in your choice of alloy.

  • Three alloys on the RX form: high yellow, high noble white and noble white
  • Often prescribed for posterior teeth where a metal restoration is preferred

Frequently asked questions

Need a specific answer? Contact the lab team directly.

Talk with the lab
Can I coordinate shade preferences?

Contact the lab about shade preferences. Custom shade appointments can be arranged by phone.

Where do I find the laboratory RX?

The current prescription form is available on the RX Form page.

How long does it take?

Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 days. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before scheduling the patient.

Is anything extra needed for anterior cases?

Yes. Our RX form asks for a pre-op or study model with all anterior cases.

How do I tell the lab the shade?

Write the shade, and the stump shade where relevant, on the RX form. For shade-critical cases you can also send photographs to photos@dentalconceptssf.com or book a custom shade appointment.

Can I send a digital scan?

Yes. Tick “Digital imp scan” on the RX form. We do not have a public upload portal, so contact the lab to confirm how to send the files.

Can my patient visit the lab for shade matching?

Yes. Call (415) 558-9717 to arrange a custom shade appointment. Visits take approximately 15 to 20 minutes.

Case-specific questions?

We do not have a public case portal. Confirm the current submission method with the lab before sending patient records.
