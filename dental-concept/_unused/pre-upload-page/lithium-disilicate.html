Full contour zirconia crowns

Milled from a single piece of zirconia, with no layered porcelain.

  • Designed digitally, then milled, sintered and hand-finished
  • Often prescribed for posterior teeth where strength comes first

Layered porcelain crowns

Porcelain built up by hand over a zirconia core, for control over translucency.

  • Color, translucency and surface character vary across the tooth
  • Often prescribed for front teeth and shade-critical cases

Talk with the lab
Is E.max the same as lithium disilicate?

E.max is the name many dentists use for lithium disilicate, a strong glass ceramic. Our RX form lists it as “Lithium Disilicate”, and our E.max dental lab work covers veneers, inlays, onlays and crowns.

Which restorations can you make in lithium disilicate?

Veneers, inlays, onlays and crowns. Note which one you need on the RX form, along with the shade and stump shade.

Why does the RX form ask for a stump shade?

Lithium disilicate is translucent, so the color of the prepared tooth can show through the restoration. Knowing the stump shade lets the technician plan for it.

How long do E.max crowns take?

Our E.max crowns lab works to the timeline on our RX form: 10 in-lab working days for 1 to 3 units and for 4 or more. Call the lab about cases of 8 units or more.

Should I choose E.max or zirconia for a crown?

E.max is a common choice for front teeth and premolars where translucency matters. Full contour zirconia is often preferred for molars and heavy biters. The call is yours, and we’re happy to talk through a specific case.

Is anything extra needed for anterior cases?

Yes. Our RX form asks for a pre-op or study model with every anterior case, along with the shade and stump shade.

Can I book a custom shade visit?

Yes. Call (415) 558-9717 to book. Visits take about 15 to 20 minutes at our lab on 9th Street.
