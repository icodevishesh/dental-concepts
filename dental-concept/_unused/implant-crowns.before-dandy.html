Overview

About implant crowns

What it is

An implant crown restores a single implant. Ours are screw-retained: the crown is fixed to the implant with a screw through an access hole, so it can be removed by the clinician later if needed.

Options on the RX form

You can ask for the crown lab cemented to its abutment as one piece with an access hole, or supplied uncemented as two pieces. You can also request angulated screw access where it is possible.

What to send

A completed RX with the implant system and size, the shade, and your impression or scan. For digital cases, our RX form asks for a full arch gingival scan without the healing abutment.

Our RX form lists implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before scheduling the patient.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Need a specific answer? Contact the lab team directly.

Talk with the lab
How should I confirm implant-system details?

Contact the laboratory with your case plan and ask what implant-system information and records it needs.

Can I download the lab prescription?

Yes. The RX form is available on the RX Form page.

How long does it take?

Our RX form lists implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before scheduling the patient.

What do digital implant cases need?

A full arch gingival scan without the healing abutment, as noted on our RX form, plus the implant system and size.

Can the lab supply the implant parts?

Yes. Tick “Lab to provide parts” on the RX form and write in the implant system and size.

Can the screw access be angled?

The RX form has an option for angulated screw access where it is possible. Whether it is possible depends on the implant system and position.

How do I tell the lab the shade?

Write the shade, and the stump shade where relevant, on the RX form. For shade-critical cases you can also send photographs to photos@dentalconceptssf.com or book a custom shade appointment.

Case-specific questions?

We do not have a public case portal. Confirm the current submission method with the lab before sending patient records.
