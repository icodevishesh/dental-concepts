FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Need a specific answer? Contact the lab team directly.

Talk with the lab
What dental lab services does Dental Concepts offer?

We make full contour zirconia, layered porcelain, lithium disilicate (E.max) and gold crowns, plus veneers, inlays and onlays. For implants, we make full arch and All-on-X prostheses, implant crowns, custom abutments and temporaries. Digital wax-ups, model printing and custom shades round out the list.

Do you make crowns and bridges?

Yes. Our work runs from a single crown to a full arch bridge. Call the lab to talk through material options for multi-unit cases before you send the RX.

How long do lab services take?

Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 in-lab working days and implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks. Full contour zirconia is quickest, at 7 days for one to three units. Weekends, holidays and shipping time aren’t counted. See the turnaround table.

Is rush service available?

Yes, on most products, for up to 4 units. Pre-schedule it at (415) 558-9716. Surcharges start at $100 per unit for cases needed within 7 business days.

Which digital scans do you accept?

We accept digital impressions from iTero, Trios and similar scanners. Tick “Digital imp scan” on the RX form, and contact the lab to confirm how to send the files.

Where can I get the RX form?

Download the current two-page RX form from the RX form page or the Quick links menu at the top of any page.
