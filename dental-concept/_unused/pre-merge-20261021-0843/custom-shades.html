How it works

From booking to seat, in three steps.

Three steps, with a technician you can talk to at every one of them.

Book

Call to book a visit

Call (415) 558-9717 to book, and tick “Custom shade” on the RX form so the case and the visit are linked.

Visit

About 15 to 20 minutes at the lab

Your patient visits us at 244 9th Street, San Francisco, Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and the technician looks at the tooth in person.

Seat

A restoration that matches its neighbours

The technician who saw the tooth builds the restoration. Confirm timing for your case with the lab.
