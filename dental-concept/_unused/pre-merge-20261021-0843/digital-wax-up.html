How it works

From prescription to seat, with no guesswork.

Three steps, with a technician you can talk to at every one of them.

Prescribe

Frequently asked questions

Talk with the lab
What records are needed for a wax up?

Contact the laboratory to confirm which files, records, and instructions are required for your case.

How do I begin?

Review the RX form and contact the lab to confirm its current case-submission method.

How long does it take?

Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 days. Confirm timing for your case with the lab before scheduling the patient.

Can I send a digital scan?

Yes. Tick “Digital imp scan” on the RX form. We do not have a public upload portal, so contact the lab to confirm how to send the files.

Can I get a printed model of the wax up?

Ask the lab when you send the case. See model printing.

What records are most useful?

A full arch scan or models of both arches, the bite, and photographs of the smile.

Case-specific questions?

We do not have a public case portal. Confirm the current submission method with the lab before sending patient records.
