Case journey

How a case moves through the lab

  1. ReceivedThe case is unpacked, checked against the RX form and logged.
  2. DesignThe restoration is planned, digitally or by hand, to your prescription.
  3. ProductionThe work is milled, printed, pressed or cast, then built up and stained.
  4. Quality checkFit, contacts, occlusion and shade are checked before it leaves.
  5. ShippedThe case is packed and sent back to your practice.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Need a specific answer? Contact the lab team directly.

Talk with the lab
Is there an online tracking portal?

No. Case status comes directly from the lab team, by email or phone.

What should I include in a status request?

The RX or case number, the doctor and practice name, and the date the case was sent. Please leave out patient names.

How long does a case usually take?

Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 days and implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks. Confirm timing for your case with the lab.

Can I change a case that is already in progress?

Call the lab as soon as possible. What can change depends on the stage the case has reached.

New doctor or case question?

Email the lab team and ask for current case submission instructions.
