**Q: What does Dental Concepts SF make?**  
Dental Concepts SF is a dental lab in California, based in San Francisco, that makes full contour zirconia crowns, layered porcelain crowns, lithium disilicate (E.max) veneers, inlays, onlays and crowns, and gold crowns. We also make full arch and All-on-X prostheses, implant crowns, custom abutments, temporaries, digital wax-ups, printed models and custom shades.

**Q: Where is your dental laboratory?**  
Our dental laboratory is at 244 9th Street, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA. We're open Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. Call 1-800-700-LABS (5227) to reach the team.

**Q: How long does a case take?**  
Our RX form lists restorations at 10 to 15 days and implant cases at 3 to 4 weeks. Full contour zirconia is quicker, at 7 in-lab days for one to three units. Working days don't include weekends, holidays or shipping, so confirm timing with the lab before you schedule the patient.

**Q: Do you offer rush service?**  
Yes, on most products, for up to 4 units. Pre-schedule it with the Lab Manager or Implant Coordinator at (415) 558-9716. Cases needed within 7 business days carry a \$100 per unit surcharge, with an additional \$100 per unit within 5 days. Anything under 3 days needs management approval and a quote.

**Q: Can I send a digital scan?**  
Yes. Check "Digital imp scan" on the RX form. We work with scans from iTero, Trios and similar systems. Digital implant cases need a full arch gingival scan without the healing abutment. Contact the lab to confirm how to send your files.

**Q: How do I book a custom shade appointment?**  
Call (415) 558-9717. Shade visits take about 15 to 20 minutes at our lab on 9th Street. When your patient can't come in, email shade photos to photos@dentalconceptssf.com instead.

**Q: What warranty do your restorations carry?**  
We repair or replace a failed final fixed restoration at no charge for materials and workmanship for up to one year (restrictions apply). Provisionals and auxiliary services are covered for six months, and implant abutments follow the implant company's guidelines. The restoration needs to be returned to the lab.

**Q: How can a new doctor open an account?**  
Email accounts@dentalconceptssf.com or use our contact form. Then download the RX form, and you're ready to send your first case.

**Q: Are your restorations made in the USA?**  
Yes. We design, make and finish every restoration at our California dental lab on 9th Street in San Francisco. Cases are never sent overseas.

**Q: What is a full contour zirconia crown?**  
A full contour zirconia crown, also called a monolithic zirconia crown, is milled from one solid piece of zirconia with no porcelain layered on top. At our zirconia dental lab, we design full contour zirconia dental crowns digitally, mill them on Roland equipment, sinter them and finish each one by hand.

**Q: How long do full contour zirconia crowns take?**  
Our RX form lists them at 7 in-lab working days, for 1 to 3 units and for 4 or more. Call the lab about cases of 8 units or more. Weekends, holidays and shipping time aren't counted.

**Q: Should I choose full contour or layered zirconia?**  
Full contour zirconia suits back teeth where strength comes first, since there's no layered porcelain to chip. Layered zirconia gives the ceramist more control over translucency on front teeth. Compare both on our layered porcelain crowns page.

**Q: Can I send a digital scan?**  
Yes. Check "Digital imp scan" on the RX form. We accept scans from iTero, Trios and similar systems. Contact the lab to confirm how to send the files.

**Q: How do I tell the lab the shade?**  
Write the shade, and the stump shade where relevant, on the RX form. For shade-critical cases, check "Custom Shade", then email photos to photos@dentalconceptssf.com or book a custom shade appointment at (415) 558-9717.

**Q: Is anything extra needed for anterior cases?**  
Yes. Our RX form asks for a pre-op or study model with every anterior case, along with the shade and stump shade.

**Q: What does the warranty cover?**  
If a placed final fixed restoration fails within one year, we repair or replace it with no charge for materials and workmanship once it's returned to us (restrictions apply). We also guarantee the fit to the die or cast model, provided the tooth was reduced to the manufacturer's recommendations.

**Q: What are layered porcelain dental crowns?**  
Layered porcelain crowns have porcelain built up by hand, in layers, over a milled zirconia core. They're also called porcelain-fused-to-zirconia (PFZ) crowns, and they appear as "Zirconia (Layered)" on our RX form.

**Q: When should I prescribe a layered porcelain crown?**  
They're a common choice for front teeth, single centrals and other shade-critical cases, where translucency and surface character matter most. For molars in patients who clench or grind, full contour zirconia is often the safer pick.

**Q: How long do layered porcelain crowns take?**  
Our RX form lists layered zirconia at 10 in-lab working days for 1 to 3 units and for 4 or more. An esthetic crown with a custom shade appointment takes 15 days, as does a single central when the patient attends the shade visit promptly.

**Q: Can my patient visit the lab for shade matching?**  
Yes. Check "Custom Shade" on the RX form and call (415) 558-9717 to book an appointment at 244 9th Street. Visits take about 15 to 20 minutes.

**Q: Is anything extra needed for anterior cases?**  
Yes. Our RX form asks for a pre-op or study model with every anterior case, along with the shade and stump shade.

**Q: Can I send a digital scan?**  
Yes. Check "Digital imp scan" on the RX form. We accept scans from iTero, Trios and similar systems. Contact the lab to confirm how to send the files.

**Q: Can I rush a layered porcelain crown?**  
Rush service is available on most products for up to 4 units, when you pre-schedule it at (415) 558-9716. Cases needed within 7 business days carry a \$100 per unit surcharge.
